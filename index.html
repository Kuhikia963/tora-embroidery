Our story

One idea: a stitched piece should feel personal.

Tora Embroidery is built around craftsmanship and quality. Every design starts on paper, moves to the hoop and is stitched by hand, thread by thread.

We would rather make fewer pieces that are made well than many that are made quickly.

What you get

Customer trust

Clear communication from first consultation to delivery.

How a piece is made

From sketch to finish

  1. Sketch

    Each design starts as a drawing, then is traced onto the fabric.

  2. Hoop

    The fabric is stretched taut so every stitch lies flat and even.

  3. Stitch

    Satin stitch, stem stitch and French knots are worked by hand.

  4. Finish

    The piece is pressed, checked and made up into its final form.
